About

A little more context.

I grew up in the Chicago suburbs, went to Tulane in New Orleans, and recently traded both for Los Angeles.

I’m naturally curious and tend to rotate through hobbies—I recently tried ceramics, I’m currently learning to sew, and surfing is next on my list. Outside of work, I’m usually vintage shopping, trying a new restaurant, taking a Pilates, yoga, or barre class, watching a movie or getting into a new show, or paying way too much attention to the brands and trends popping up around me.

I’m especially interested in consumer behavior, fashion, beauty, and the intersection of creativity and technology.
